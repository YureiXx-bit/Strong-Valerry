Server Rules

Please read and follow these rules to keep our community fair, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.

Rule 01 Respect All Players

Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or toxic behavior is strictly forbidden.

Rule 02 No Cheating

Using hacks, exploits, bugs, or unfair modifications will result in severe punishment.

Rule 03 No Griefing

Destroying or stealing other players' builds without permission is not allowed.

Rule 04 Appropriate Language

Keep chat clean and friendly. Excessive swearing or offensive language is prohibited.

Rule 05 Follow Staff Instructions

Staff decisions are final. Ignoring or arguing with staff may lead to punishment.

Rule 06 No Advertising

Advertising other servers, websites, or services without permission is not allowed.

⚖️ Punishment System

⚠️ Warning

Minor rule breaks will result in a warning. Repeated warnings lead to stronger punishment.

🔇 Mute

Chat-related rule violations may result in a temporary or permanent mute.

⛔ Ban

Severe or repeated violations will result in a temporary or permanent ban.

By playing on this server, you agree to follow all rules. Rules may change at any time.
